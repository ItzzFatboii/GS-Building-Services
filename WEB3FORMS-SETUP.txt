G.S BUILDING SERVICES – DIRECT WEBSITE LEADS

The website is configured to send estimator leads directly to:
g.sbuildingserviceswv@gmail.com

Provider: Web3Forms
Docs: https://docs.web3forms.com/getting-started/installation

ONE-TIME SETUP:
1. Go to https://web3forms.com/
2. Create an access key using the business email address above.
3. Open script.js in this website package.
4. Find:
   const WEB3FORMS_ACCESS_KEY='YOUR_WEB3FORMS_ACCESS_KEY';
5. Replace the placeholder with your Web3Forms access key.
6. Save and upload the complete website.

The access key is intended for client-side use by Web3Forms; do NOT put your Gmail password,
mailbox password, or SMTP password in the website.

The owner email will receive:
- Customer name, phone, email and area/postcode
- Project type
- Customer-facing estimate
- PRIVATE INTERNAL PLANNING FIGURE
- All estimator answers
- Customer notes

The private internal planning figure is never displayed in the customer-facing result.

IMPORTANT:
The internal figure is a guide-based planning figure, not a guaranteed material cost or formal quote.
You should still check current merchant prices, site conditions, waste, access, drainage, structural work
and other project-specific costs before issuing your formal price.

The direct email service requires the one-time Web3Forms access key because a static website cannot
safely contain your mailbox password or run its own mail server.
